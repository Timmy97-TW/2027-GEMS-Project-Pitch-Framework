GEMS Taiwan · iGEM 2027

一個題目要研究到什麼程度，才能在二月定案 How much must you know in February to commit a project to October

iGEM 不是比誰的想法最好，是比誰的想法到十月能被證明。這份文件把「定案」拆成十道門，每一道門問一個問題、要求一份在二月就該存在的證據，並且允許回答「過不了」。後半是完整範例：把 2026 年的 ReLEAF 專案倒回二月，按同一套架構寫成一份提案，並在每一格旁邊放上後來真正發生的事。

iGEM is not won by the best idea. It is won by the idea that can still be proved in October. This document breaks the February commitment into ten gates. Each gate asks one question, demands one piece of evidence that must already exist, and is allowed to return a no. The second half is a worked example: the 2026 ReLEAF project wound back to February, written as a proposal against this framework, with what actually happened set beside each answer.

01 定案不是選題，是承諾一組數字 Committing is not choosing a topic. It is promising a set of numbers

二月定案時，你不是在說「這個題目很有趣」。你是在說：十月我們會交出這幾個數字，每個數字有對照組、有重複數、有儀器，而我們現在就知道做這些數字要花多少錢、用什麼設備、誰來做、哪一步失敗全盤皆輸。

When you commit in February you are not saying "this topic is interesting". You are saying: in October we will hand over these numbers, each with a control, a replicate count and a named instrument, and we already know what they cost, which equipment they need, who runs them, and which single step takes the whole project down if it fails.

一個無法在二月寫出「我們要量什麼、跟什麼比、量幾次」的題目，不是還沒想清楚，是還不能做。

A project that cannot state in February what it will measure, against what control, and how many times, is not an unfinished idea. It is an idea that cannot yet be started.

這份架構的唯一主張The one claim this framework makes

為什麼用「門」而不是「計畫書」Why gates and not a plan

計畫書會把每一節都寫滿，因為空白看起來像沒做功課。門不一樣：門可以關上。十道門裡任何一道答不出來，答案就是「這個題目二月不要定案」，而不是「這一節先寫個大概」。高中隊伍的時間只有八個月，過不了的門在二月攔下來，比在八月才發現便宜太多。

A plan document fills every heading, because a blank looks like homework not done. A gate can close. If any one of the ten has no answer, the answer is "do not commit this project in February", not "leave that section vague for now". A high-school team has eight working months. A gate that fails in February is far cheaper than the same gate failing in August.

三種結果，不是兩種Three outcomes, not two
  • 通過　證據齊、數字可達、資源夠。可以定案。
  • 保留　缺的是可以在四週內補上的東西（一份報價、一次訪談、一次預試）。給它一個日期，不給它一個「之後再說」。
  • 否決　缺的是八個月補不回來的東西（設備、法規時程、生物安全等級、關鍵試劑無法取得）。換題目。
  • Pass. Evidence in hand, numbers reachable, resources sufficient. Commit it.
  • Hold. What is missing can be closed in four weeks: a quote, an interview, a pilot run. Give it a date, never a "we will sort that out later".
  • Reject. What is missing cannot be closed in eight months: equipment, a regulatory clock, a biosafety level, a reagent nobody will sell you. Change the project.

02 四個階段，十道門 Four phases, ten gates

順序是刻意的。前三道門問「值不值得做」，答錯只是浪費一季；中間三道問「我們做不做得到」，答錯整季歸零；後面四道問「範圍與收割」，答錯會做完卻拿不到分。先過前面，再談後面。

The order is deliberate. The first three gates ask whether the project is worth doing; getting them wrong costs a season of interest. The middle three ask whether this team can actually do it; getting them wrong costs the season itself. The last four are scope and harvest; getting them wrong means finishing the work and scoring nothing for it.

G4 and G5 carry 30 points between them, on purpose. They are the two gates a high-school team most often skips and most often pays for in September: the critical path scheduled late, and every number measured once.

G10 不是收尾工作，因為 iGEM 的規則不允許G10 is not a tidying-up job, because the rules do not allow it

2026 年的規則：一隊最多只能申請三個特別獎（Special Awards），三個之中至少一個來自 General Bio Engineering 類、至少一個來自 Specializations 類，而這三個就是金牌的條件——三個都要達到評分門檻才拿金牌，選不滿三個直接沒有金牌。所以「我們瞄哪三個獎」是二月的設計決定，它會往回改掉實驗怎麼做；而且每個獎都有自己的資格限制與指定的標準網址，做了但放錯頁面不會被評分。

The 2026 rule: a team may apply for at most three Special Awards, at least one from General Bio Engineering and at least one from Specializations, and those three are the Gold medal criterion — all three must meet the performance standard, and selecting fewer than three means no Gold. So "which three are we aiming at" is a February design decision that reaches back into how the experiments run. Each award also has its own eligibility rules and its own designated Standard URL; work filed on the wrong page is not evaluated.

03 二月門檻：研究到什麼程度算夠 The February bar

「研究到什麼程度」這個問題，答案不是讀幾篇論文，是在二月那天桌上要有哪些實體的東西。下面這張表是門檻，不是目標。

"How much research is enough" is not answered in papers read. It is answered by what is physically on the table on the day of the decision. The list below is a floor, not a target.

3支撐整個題目的錨論文，其中一篇有我們打算重現的圖anchor papers the project stands on, one of them with a figure you intend to reproduce
3已經做完（不是排程中）的利害關係人訪談，其中一位是潛在使用者stakeholder conversations already done, not scheduled, one of them a potential user
1學生自己手上跑成功的預試：菌長起來、轉形成功、儀器校正過pilot the students ran themselves: the chassis grew, a transformation worked, an instrument was calibrated
1含 30% 預備金的成本模型，以及最長交期那一項的報價單cost model with 30% contingency, plus a quote for the longest-lead item

完整的二月門檻清單在工具包，共二十二項，分成文獻、序列與零件、預試、數字、資源、人文、範圍、收割八類。十道門那一頁則逐門寫出「二月前必須存在什麼」與「紅旗」。

The full bar — twenty-two items under literature, sequences and parts, pilot work, numbers, resources, practices, scope and harvest — is in the toolkit. The gates page states, gate by gate, what must exist by February and which answers are red flags.

04 三條判斷規則 Three rules for judging a pitch

PK 的時候，老師和學生都會對自己的題目太寬容。這三條規則是用來替代直覺的。

At the PK everyone is too kind to their own project. These three rules stand in for instinct.

規則一Rule one

一句話預期結果The one-sentence result

題目要能填滿這個句子：「在 Y 條件下，X 讓 Z 改變 N 倍，對照組是 W，n＝3。」填不滿的題目不是不好，是還沒變成一個實驗。

The project must fill this sentence: "Under condition Y, X changes Z by N-fold against control W, with n = 3." A project that cannot fill it is not a bad project. It is not yet an experiment.

紅旗Red flag

句子裡出現「改善」、「提升效率」、「更永續」而沒有單位。

The sentence contains "improve", "more efficient" or "more sustainable" and no unit.

規則二Rule two

脊椎與肋骨Spine and ribs

一個題目只能有一根脊椎：一條從輸入到輸出、每一節都要把東西交給下一節的鏈。肋骨可以多，但每一根肋骨都要掛在脊椎上。獨立子系統每多一個，分數就往下走——不是因為野心不好，是因為八個月只夠餵養一條鏈。

A project gets one spine: a chain from input to output where every link must hand a real output to the next. Ribs can be many, but every rib hangs off the spine. Each additional independent subsystem lowers the score — not because ambition is bad, but because eight months feed one chain.

紅旗Red flag

提案裡有兩個系統可以各自單獨發表，中間沒有交付物。

Two subsystems in the pitch could each be published alone, with no handover between them.

規則三Rule three

十月的驗屍先做Pre-mortem first

提案人必須自己回答：「假設十月這個題目失敗了，最可能的原因是什麼？」然後說出那個原因要用哪個實驗、在哪個日期排除。學生挑戰老師題目時，這一題最好用。

The proposer answers, in their own words: "Suppose this project has failed by October. What is the most likely reason?" Then names the experiment that rules that reason out, and its date. This is the sharpest question a student can put to an instructor's project.

紅旗Red flag

回答是「時間不夠」或「人手不足」。那是每個題目的答案，等於沒有回答。

The answer is "not enough time" or "not enough people". That is every project's answer, so it is not an answer.

05 這套架構是從 2026 的帳單上寫出來的 This framework was written off the 2026 invoice

ReLEAF 做完了一條完整的鏈，拿到了可以站得住的硬體與量測成果。下面五件事是那一季真正的代價，每一件都對應這套架構裡的一道門。完整對照在工具包的後見之明帳。

ReLEAF finished a complete chain and came away with hardware and measurement work that stands up. The five items below are what that season actually cost, and each one maps onto a gate. The full ledger is in the toolkit.

2026 發生的事What happened in 2026 二月那天本來可以攔下它的門The gate that would have caught it in February
反應器的每個時間點都是單一樣本，生物重複數為一。統計全部落在儀器上，沒有落在生物上。Every reactor time point was a single sample. The statistics live on the instrument, not on the biology. G5 量測計畫：每個主張在二月就要寫定 n，而且 n 要進耗材預算。Measurement: fix n per claim in February, and put that n into the consumables budget.
光控開關是題目的招牌，但從未在反應器裡跑過；所有反應器批次都是組成型表現。The light switch was the headline, and it never ran in the reactor. Every reactor batch was constitutive. G4 關鍵路徑：招牌功能要排在前面，並掛一個有日期的 go／no-go，不能排在最後。Critical path: the headline function goes early with a dated go/no-go, never last.
幾個感測器裝上了、但沒有被測試過，頁面得用「裝了但未測」這種句子描述。Several sensors were fitted and never tested, and the pages had to say "fitted, not tested". G7 硬體範圍：每一個零件都要寫出「少了它哪個量測做不到」，答不出來就不要裝。Hardware scope: every component states which measurement is impossible without it. No answer, no part.
實驗紀錄到十月才從聊天室和投影片倒回來重建。The notebook was reconstructed in October out of chat logs and slide decks. G6 資源：紀錄是人力項目。每週一人值班，把當週整理成一頁，從第一週開始。Resources: record-keeping is a staffed role. One person a week turns that week into one page, from week one.
同一台硬體在兩頁上有兩個價錢，兩頁都是對的，因為沒人是那個數字的主人。The same hardware carried two prices on two pages. Both were defensible, because no one owned the number. G10 收割：每個對外數字指定一位主人，數字只能從一個檔案出來。Harvest: every published number gets one owner and one source file.
這不是檢討 2026，是定價 2027Not a post-mortem, a price list

上面每一行的修法都要花錢或花人力：n＝3 要三倍的 ELISA 套組，光控 go／no-go 要提早兩個月拿到菌株，每週紀錄要一個固定的人。二月定案時這些都要在預算裡，否則十月又會變成同樣的句子。

Every fix above costs money or people: n = 3 triples the ELISA kits, an early light-gate go/no-go needs the strain two months sooner, a weekly record needs a named person. Those costs belong in the February budget, or October writes the same sentences again.
