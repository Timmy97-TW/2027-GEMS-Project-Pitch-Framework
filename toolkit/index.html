01 二月門檻，二十二項 The February bar, twenty-two items

定案那天，這二十二項要在桌上。「在桌上」是指可以遞給別人看的東西：一張表、一條曲線、一封回信、一份報價。計畫不算。

On the day of the decision these twenty-two things are on the table. "On the table" means something you can hand to someone: a table, a curve, a reply, a quote. A plan does not count.

文獻與前人Literature and prior art

  • 1　三篇錨論文Three anchor papers每一篇寫出「我們相信它哪一張圖」與「它不保證什麼」。另有 40–60 篇略讀、10–15 篇細讀的清單。Each with the figure you trust and what it does not promise. Plus a list of 40–60 skimmed and 10–15 read closely.
  • 2　iGEM 前人表，附連結The prior-iGEM table, with links四個關鍵字各掃過 Registry 與歷年 wiki；每列是隊伍、年份、證明了什麼、沒證明什麼、我們因此改什麼。不可憑記憶。Four keyword sweeps across the Registry and the past wikis. One row per team: year, what they proved, what they did not, what you change. Never from memory.
  • 3　新意一句話，與「不宣稱」清單The novelty sentence and the "we do not claim" list新意要標明種類：新部件、新方法、新整合、新場景、新成本結構。挑一種。Label its kind: new part, new method, new integration, new setting, new cost structure. Pick one.

序列與零件Sequences and parts

  • 4　建構清單The construct list每個質體寫出骨架、啟動子、RBS、CDS、標籤、終止子，每一項附 Registry 編號或 NCBI accession。Per plasmid: backbone, promoter, RBS, CDS, tags, terminator, each with a Registry number or an NCBI accession.
  • 5　組裝路線已選，合成報價已拿到Assembly route chosen, synthesis quote in handGolden Gate 或 Gibson 或其他，以及密碼子最佳化的決定。報價要有交期。Golden Gate, Gibson or other, plus the codon-optimisation decision. The quote carries a lead time.
  • 6　骨架與底盤在手上或已下單Backbone and chassis in the lab or ordered「向某教授索取」只有在對方已經回信同意時才算。"Requesting it from a professor" counts only once they have replied yes.

預試（學生自己動手跑過的）Pilot work the students ran themselves

  • 7　底盤在我們的培養箱裡長起來的生長曲線A growth curve of the chassis in our own incubator別人實驗室的曲線不算。這條曲線同時是儀器、培養基與操作的健康檢查。Someone else's curve does not count. This curve also health-checks the instrument, the media and the technique.
  • 8　一次成功的轉形，由學生操作One successful transformation, done by a student用陽性對照質體即可。目的是確認流程走得通、而且人會做。A positive-control plasmid is fine. The point is that the workflow runs and a person can do it.
  • 9　一台儀器的校正曲線，由學生做，曲線留著One instrument calibrated by a student, with the curve kept校正品是什麼要寫出來。沒有校正品的數字沒有軸。Name the standard. A number with no standard has no axis.

數字Numbers

  • 10　量測表填滿，五到八列The measurement table, five to eight rows欄位：主張、數字與單位、對照、n、儀器與校正、統計、完成日、邊界。對照欄不可有空格。Columns: claim, number and unit, control, n, instrument and calibration, statistic, done-by, boundary. No blanks in the control column.
  • 11　n × 單次成本的總額，與耗材預算對得上n × unit cost, summed, reconciled with the consumables budget買不起就減列數，不要減 n。這是整份清單最重要的一條。If it is unaffordable, cut rows, not n. This is the most important line on the list.
  • 12　預先登記的決定The pre-registration decision哪幾個數字要在實驗前把預期值寫進版本控制。成本接近零，而且被推翻的預測是最強的證據。Which numbers get expected values committed to version control before the run. It costs almost nothing, and an overturned prediction is the strongest evidence there is.

資源Resources

  • 13　設備三類清單The three-class equipment list已經有／要借（具名單位、具名聯絡人、已經問過）／要買或做（有價格、有交期）。每一項註明它卡住哪一列量測。Have / borrow (named institution, named contact, already asked) / buy or build (price and lead time). Each noting which measurement it blocks.
  • 14　按月的耗材預算，含 nA month-by-month consumables budget carrying the n合成 DNA、抗體與套組、培養與塑膠三項各自一行。運費與關稅也要一行。Synthesised DNA, antibodies and kits, culture and plastics each get a row. So do shipping and duty.
  • 15　30% 預備金，寫成一行A 30% contingency, written as a line口頭說「應該還有空間」不算。Saying "there should be room" does not count.
  • 16　生物安全等級已確認、White List 已逐項核過、簽核人已具名Biosafety level settled, White List checked item by item, signer named等級與空間不符是否決項。高中隊伍只能使用 iGEM White List 上的生物、零件與操作：不做動物、不做人體樣本、不碰 BSL-2 以上。清單外的生物會在六月的安全表單被攔下來。A mismatch between the level and the space is a reject. High-school teams may only use organisms, parts and activities on the iGEM White List: no animals, no human samples, nothing at BSL-2 or above. An organism off the list is caught by the June safety form.

人與紀錄People and the record

  • 17　每個實驗兩人會做，紀錄值班表已排Two people per experiment, record rota set一個人會做等於那條線有病假與考試週風險。值班是每週一人把那一週變成一頁，從第一週開始。One owner means that line has a sick-day and exam-week risk. The rota is one person a week turning that week into one page, from week one.

人文與法規Practices and regulation

  • 18　三場已經做完的對話Three conversations already held每場留下「他說了什麼／我們因此改了什麼」兩行。其中一位是潛在使用者，不能全是教授。Each leaving two lines: what they said, what you changed. One of the three is a potential user; they cannot all be professors.
  • 19　法規路徑、圍堵句子、安全表單負責人Regulatory route, containment sentence, safety-form owner類別名稱、法源名稱、大約的審查時程；工程菌離不離開容器，以及擋住它的是什麼；安全與保安表單的負責人與送交日期（照當季公告）。The category, the statute, the approximate clock; whether the cells leave the vessel and what stops them; an owner and a date for the Safety & Security forms, per the current season's deadlines.

範圍與收割Scope and harvest

  • 20　不做清單The not-doing list這一季明確不做的硬體、軟體與實驗分支。寫出來的那一刻，範圍才真的存在。The hardware, software and experimental branches this season will not do. Scope exists only once it is written.
  • 21　風險表、三個停損條件、縮小版題目Risk table, three kill criteria, de-scoped version停損條件各帶日期與數字門檻。縮小版是「只剩一半時間與資源時的最小可交付成果」。Each kill criterion with a date and a numeric threshold. The de-scoped version is the minimum deliverable on half the time and half the resources.
  • 22　四個 go／no-go、三個特別獎名額、論文一句話、數字主人Four go/no-go dates, the three Special Award slots, the novelty sentence, number owners獎牌條件逐條對應到工作項目、負責人與標準網址（照當季公布的版本）。三個特別獎名額要跨 General Bio Engineering 與 Specializations 兩類，逐個核對資格限制——這三個就是金牌的條件，選不滿三個沒有金牌。論文附 4–6 張圖的清單。每個對外數字一位主人、一個來源檔案。Medal criteria mapped line by line to work items, owners and Standard URLs, against the current season's published version. The three Special Award slots span General Bio Engineering and Specializations, with eligibility checked one by one — those three are the Gold criterion, and fewer than three means no Gold. The paper comes with its four-to-six figure list. Every published number has one owner and one source file.
否決項：這四項缺一就不要定案Rejects: missing any one of these four means do not commit
  • 第 11 項（n × 成本對得上預算）——缺它等於承諾了做不起的數據。
  • 第 16 項（生物安全等級）——缺它等於整季可能被迫停擺。
  • 第 6 項（骨架與底盤在手或已下單）——缺它等於關鍵路徑還沒開始就落後。
  • 第 21 項（停損條件）——缺它等於沒有人敢把關鍵實驗排在前面。
  • Item 11, n × cost reconciled: without it you have promised data you cannot afford.
  • Item 16, biosafety level: without it the season can be stopped outright.
  • Item 6, backbone and chassis in hand or ordered: without it the critical path is behind before it starts.
  • Item 21, kill criteria: without them nobody dares schedule the decisive experiment first.

02 一頁提案模板 The one-page pitch

完整提案是 12–15 頁。但 PK 當天先交一頁，而且只唸這一頁。老師的題目與學生的題目用同一張模板，PK 才公平。

The full pitch runs 12 to 15 pages. On PK day you hand over one page and you read only that page. The instructors' projects and the students' projects use the same template, which is what makes the PK fair.

兩條修正項Two adjustments

加分Bonus

一句話預期結果　＋5A one-sentence expected result　+5

主張能填滿「在 Y 條件下，X 讓 Z 改變 N 倍，對照組是 W」且 N 有一個有理由的預估值，加 5 分。預估值錯了不扣分；說不出預估值才扣分。

If the claim fills "under Y, X changes Z by N-fold against control W" and N has a reasoned estimate, add 5. Being wrong about N costs nothing. Having no N does.

扣分Penalty

戰線扣分　每多一個獨立子系統 −4Breadth penalty　−4 per independent subsystem

脊椎之外，每一個可以單獨存在的子系統（另一套硬體、另一個物種、另一個獨立軟體產品）扣 4 分。2026 的題目按這條規則會扣 8 到 12 分。

Beyond the spine, each subsystem that could stand alone — another instrument, another species, another standalone software product — costs 4. The 2026 project loses 8 to 12 points under this rule.

合議時只問三個問題Three questions in the discussion
  1. 哪一道門的分數，大家差距最大？差距大的地方就是提案講不清楚的地方。
  2. 如果這個題目要補一樣東西才能定案，是哪一樣，四週內補得完嗎？
  3. 提案人的驗屍回答，房間裡有人不同意嗎？
  4. Which gate's scores are furthest apart? The spread marks where the pitch is unclear.
  5. If this project needs one more thing before it can be committed, what is it, and can it close in four weeks?
  6. Does anyone in the room disagree with the proposer's pre-mortem?

04 2026 後見之明帳 The 2026 hindsight ledger

五筆帳，每一筆都寫出要花什麼才能避免。修法不是「下次要更小心」，修法是錢、人或時程。

Five entries, each with the price of avoiding it. The fix is never "be more careful next time". The fix is money, people or schedule.

發生的事What happened 門Gate 修法的代價What the fix costs
貴的實驗都是 n＝1Every expensive experiment at n = 1植物拿到 n＝9 與 p 值，反應器每個時間點只有一個樣本plants returned n = 9 with p-values; every reactor time point was a single sample G5 三倍的 ELISA 套組與試劑，加上一整個「無質體對照批次」的培養基與時間。要在二月編進預算，否則整季沒有人會花一次批次做對照。Three times the ELISA kits and reagents, plus the media and the days for one whole plasmid-free control run. Budget it in February or nobody will ever spend a run on a control.
招牌功能從未整合The headline never integrated光控只在光板上測過，每個反應器批次都是組成型表現light control tested only on the light plate; every reactor run was constitutive G4 提早兩個月拿到質體（一月就要索取或下單），以及三月底一個敢執行的 go／no-go。成本是時程，不是錢。Getting the plasmid two months earlier — requested or ordered in January — and a go/no-go at the end of March that the team dares to act on. The cost is schedule, not money.
裝了但沒測的零件Parts fitted and never tested溶氧探針無法維持校正、溫度探針無回應、沒有液位感測a dissolved-oxygen probe that would not hold calibration, an unresponsive temperature probe, no level sensing G7 不花錢，只要在二月砍掉。每個零件問一句「少了它哪一列量測做不到」，答不出來就不裝。省下的錢與時間可以拿去補 n。Costs nothing; it is a February deletion. Ask each component which measurement dies without it, and if there is no answer, do not fit it. The money and hours saved go into replicates.
紀錄事後重建The record rebuilt afterwards十月從聊天室與投影片倒回來拼出實驗紀錄the notebook reassembled in October from chat logs and slide decks G6 每週一人、一小時，從第一週開始。聊天室可以留著當溝通工具，但實驗紀錄要有固定格式的貼文與一個整理的人。重建花掉的時間遠多於一整季的值班。One person, one hour a week, from week one. Keep the chat for talking, and give experiments a fixed post format and someone who tidies. The rebuild cost far more hours than a season of rota.

也要記得 2026 做對了什麼，因為那些要照抄What 2026 got right, which is what to copy
  • 預先登記。在量測之前把預期值寫進版本控制，然後公布被推翻的那幾項。成本接近零，說服力最高。
  • 把失敗留在頁面上。開放的循環、撤回的主張、兩筆自家紀錄的矛盾都寫出來，這是整份 wiki 最強的信用資產。
  • 對話真的改掉設計。底盤、圍堵策略、植物模式、作物、商業模式五項轉折都能指名日期與那句話。
  • 自製儀器有驗證路徑。兩種獨立質量標準品、與商用儀器的斜率與截距檢定、以及外部的人照指南自建成功。
  • Pre-registration. Expected values committed to version control before measuring, and the overturned predictions published. Near-zero cost, highest persuasive return.
  • Failures left on the page. Open cycles, retracted claims and two contradictions between the team's own records, all written out. It is the wiki's strongest credibility asset.
  • Conversations that genuinely changed the design. Chassis, containment, plant model, crop and business model — five turns, each nameable with a date and a sentence.
  • Home-built instruments with a validation route. Two independent mass standards, a slope-and-intercept test against a commercial instrument, and outsiders who built one from the guide.

05 12 月 12 日到定案 From 12 December to the commitment

九週。訓練與題目探索並行，不要先訓練完再談題目——學生在還不會做實驗的時候讀題目 brief，問出來的問題反而比較好。

Nine weeks, with training and project exploration running together. Do not finish the training before discussing projects: students who read a brief before they can run an experiment ask better questions about it.

  • 現在 → 12 月 11 日Now → 11 Dec

    老師與留下來的學生Instructors and returning students

    2026 檢討（每人一頁：什麼做太晚、什麼做太多、什麼證據最後沒有）；1–3 份題目 brief，每份一頁，用一頁提案模板的前半；訂訓練用的菌株、質體與試劑（有些要好幾週）；建好 Discord 的頻道結構。

    The 2026 review, one page each: what ran too late, what there was too much of, which evidence never arrived. One to three project briefs, one page apiece, using the top half of the one-page template. Order the strains, plasmids and reagents for training, some of which take weeks. Set up the Discord channel structure.

  • 12 月 12 日12 Dec

    開跑日Kick-off

    第一天就把規則講完：實驗室安全、iGEM 的安全規範、AI 使用規則（AI 可以潤飾，內容由團隊撰寫，證據圖永不使用 AI，且要揭露）。不要等到寫 wiki 的時候才講。同一天發出題目 brief 與這份架構。

    Settle the rules on day one: lab safety, iGEM's safety policy, and the AI rule — AI may polish, the team authors, evidentiary figures are never AI-generated, and use is disclosed. Do not leave this until the wiki is being written. Hand out the project briefs and this framework the same day.

  • 第 1–4 週Weeks 1–4

    基本訓練，兩條線並行Basic training, two tracks in parallel

    濕實驗每週一個技術，每次都寫紀錄：無菌操作與配製培養基；PCR 與電泳；組裝、轉形與挑菌；表現與量測。乾實驗同時進行，每人做出一個小成品：一個簡單模型、一個感測器讀值、一個網頁區塊、或一次訪談。訓練的副產品是看出誰適合哪一條線。

    One wet-lab technique a week, each written up: sterile technique and media; PCR and gels; assembly, transformation and picking colonies; expression and measurement. The dry-lab track runs alongside, each student producing one small thing — a simple model, a sensor reading, a web section, or one interview. A by-product of the training is seeing who belongs on which track.

  • 第 2–7 週Weeks 2–7

    題目探索Project exploration

    學生選一份老師的 brief 深入，或自己提一個。兩種都要交 iGEM 前人表——這是最能看出投入程度的作業，而且做完之後題目會自己改形狀。每人另外挑一支過去的得獎隊伍，用固定模板分析：他們的問題、底盤、實際證明了什麼、哪裡弱。

    Students either go deep on one of the instructors' briefs or propose their own. Both routes hand in a prior-iGEM table: it is the assignment that shows commitment most clearly, and doing it changes the shape of the project by itself. Each student also analyses one past award-winning team against a fixed template: their problem, their chassis, what they actually proved, where they were weak.

  • 第 7 週Week 7

    一頁提案互評One-pagers circulated

    所有題目的一頁提案互相傳閱，每個人替每一份打一次 PK 表。這一輪的目的不是選出來，是讓提案人看到自己哪一道門講不清楚，還有兩週可以補。

    Every project's one-pager goes round and everyone scores every one. This round does not choose; it shows each proposer which gate they cannot explain, with two weeks left to fix it.

  • 第 9 週（1 月下旬）Week 9, late January

    PK 日PK day

    每個題目：唸一頁提案、回答驗屍那一題、接受提問。老師的題目也要被學生打分。評分獨立進行，再合議那三個問題。

    Per project: read the one-pager, answer the pre-mortem question, take questions. The instructors' projects are scored by the students too. Score independently, then discuss the three questions.

  • 農曆年前（2027 年除夕是 2 月 6 日）Before Lunar New Year, 6 Feb 2027

    定案Commitment

    在年前定案，寒假就能跑第一個 sprint：下單合成 DNA、索取質體、把借用的儀器時段訂下來。拖到二月中之後，那幾週就空掉了，而那幾週剛好是最長交期項目該下單的時候。

    Commit before the holiday and the first sprint runs through it: order the synthesised DNA, request the plasmids, book the borrowed instrument. Slip past mid-February and those weeks go empty — and they are exactly the weeks the longest-lead items should be ordered in.

iGEM 自己的時程，以 2026 年的形狀為例iGEM's own deadlines, in 2026's shape

日期每季不同，但形狀穩定：報名在上半年收口，安全表單分成好幾個日期，然後十月有一個「凍結日」把 wiki、判讀表單、貢獻者表、Registry 零件與軟體倉庫同時封掉，簡報影片再晚一週。2026 年的所有繳交期限都在當日 15:00 UTC（台灣時間 23:00）截止，而且官方寫明不接受延期。

The dates move each season and the shape holds: registration closes in the first half of the year, the safety forms fall on several separate dates, then a freeze day in October closes the wiki, the judging form, the attributions form, the Registry parts and the software repository at once, with the presentation video a week later. In 2026 every deliverable deadline closed at 15:00 UTC on its date, 23:00 Taiwan time, and the rules state plainly that extensions are not allowed.

一條容易漏掉的評審規則A judging rule that is easy to miss

每一項獎牌條件與獎項都對應一個指定的標準網址（Standard URL）。官方寫明評審沒有義務去看那些網址與 Registry 頁面之外的東西：「如果你把工作記在這些指定位置之外，那份工作可能不會被評分。」所以二月排計畫時，每一項工作都要同時決定它最後會出現在哪一個網址上。做了但放錯頁面，等於沒做。

Every medal criterion and award maps to a designated Standard URL. The rules state that judges are not obliged to look beyond those URLs and the relevant Registry pages: work documented elsewhere may simply not be evaluated. So in February every work item also gets a decision about which URL it will finally appear on. Done but filed in the wrong place counts as not done.
