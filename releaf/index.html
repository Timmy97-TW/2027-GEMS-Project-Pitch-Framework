這是把 2026 年的 ReLEAF 專案倒回定案前，按十道門寫成的一份完整提案。每一道門下面有兩層：二月的答案是當時應該寫出來的內容，後來是 2026 年真正量到的結果。兩者放在一起，就看得出哪些二月的承諾兌現了、哪些沒有、以及為什麼。

This is the 2026 ReLEAF project wound back to before the decision and written out against the ten gates. Each gate has two layers: the February answer, which is what should have been written at the time, and what happened, which is what 2026 actually measured. Set side by side they show which February promises were kept, which were not, and why.

00 一份提案的解剖 Anatomy of a pitch

一份定案用的提案是十五頁。下面把 ReLEAF 的那十五頁攤開：每一頁要回答什麼、寫得出那一頁之前要研究到什麼程度（用數得出來的單位，因為「多讀一點」不是一個指令）、當時實際寫了什麼、以及後來證明夠不夠。

A pitch that commits a project runs to fifteen pages. Below, ReLEAF's fifteen are laid out: what each page has to answer, how much research it takes before that page can be written — in countable units, because "read more" is not an instruction — what was actually written there, and whether it turned out to be enough.

這張圖要看的不是綠色那幾頁The green pages are not the point

兩頁從來沒有寫過（iGEM 前人評估、風險與停損），七頁寫得不夠深。這十五頁加起來，就是「一個題目在二月要研究到什麼程度」的具體答案——而缺的那幾頁，每一頁後來都用一整季的代價換回一個教訓。

Two pages were never written at all — the prior-iGEM assessment and the risk-and-kill page — and seven were written too thin. Taken together these fifteen are the concrete answer to "how much must be researched by February", and every missing page bought its lesson at the price of a season.

這一頁的讀法How to read this page

標成「後來」的所有數字都是 2026 年事後量到的，二月當下並不存在。把它們放進一份假想的二月提案裡，唯一的目的是檢查這套架構會不會提早問出對的問題。對 2027 的新題目，「後來」那一欄一律是空的——那正是重點。

Every number marked "what happened" was measured later in 2026 and did not exist in February. They sit inside a hypothetical February pitch for one purpose: to test whether this framework would have asked the right question early. For a new 2027 project that column is empty, which is the point.

G1

主張The claim

10 / 10

二月的答案The February answer

一個密閉的中空纖維灌流培養系統，可以讓工程化的 B. subtilis 把植物保護蛋白分泌到無細胞的透膜液中，其濃度足以在鹽與熱逆境下使幼苗的根長與鮮重相對無細胞培養液對照有可量測的改善，而工程菌不離開迴路。

A contained hollow-fibre perfusion culture can deliver a plant-protectant protein, secreted by engineered B. subtilis, into a cell-free permeate at a concentration that measurably improves seedling root length and fresh weight under salt and heat stress against a cell-free medium control, while the engineered cells never leave the loop.

這個主張錯的時候長什麼樣子What it looks like when the claim is wrong

  • 透膜液中偵測不到目標蛋白，或濃度與無質體對照無法區分。
  • 蛋白過得去但活性為零——膜把折疊好的酵素剪掉了，或分泌訊號把它切壞了。
  • 幼苗對透膜液與對無細胞培養液的反應相同，代表效果來自培養液成分，不是來自蛋白。
  • 透膜側長出菌落，圍堵主張失效。
  • No target protein detectable in the permeate, or a concentration indistinguishable from the plasmid-free control.
  • Protein crosses but activity is zero: the membrane kept the folded enzyme out, or the secretion signal mangled it.
  • Seedlings respond the same to the permeate and to plain cell-free medium, so the effect is the medium, not the protein.
  • Colonies grow from the permeate side, and the containment claim is gone.

三個版本Three descriptions

給評審，25 字For a judge, 25 words

把生產移到農場：密閉反應器在作物需要的那一天現做植物保護蛋白，工程菌留在膜的這一側。

Manufacturing moved to the farm: a contained reactor makes a plant protectant on the day the crop needs it, with the cells kept behind the membrane.

→
給使用者，一分鐘For a user, one minute

你現在買的生物製劑要冷鏈、會失活、而且必須在知道要不要用之前就買。這台機器放在田邊，你在手機上按下同意，它幾天內做出當季要用的劑量，你稀釋後噴。

The biologicals you buy now need a cold chain, lose activity, and must be bought before you know whether you need them. This unit stands by the field, you approve a run on your phone, it makes this season's dose in a few days, and you dilute it and spray.

後來What happened

這個主張兌現了，而且是 2026 最強的部分：反應器的透膜側在 72 小時的時間序列中每一個樣本都高於無質體對照（1.3 µg/mL），而透膜側兩次塗盤都沒有長出菌落。但主張的最後一段——「幼苗改善來自蛋白」——沒有用透膜液做完；植物實驗用的是另外製備的蛋白與菌液，不是反應器的產物。二月那句話裡的「透膜液」三個字，一直沒有在植物實驗裡出現。

The claim was kept, and it is the strongest thing 2026 produced: over a 72-hour permeate time course every sample sat above the 1.3 µg/mL plasmid-free control, and the permeate side grew no colonies on two platings. The last clause — that the seedling improvement comes from the protein — was never closed with permeate: the plant work used separately prepared protein and culture, not the reactor's own output. The word "permeate" in that February sentence never reached the plant bench.

iGEM 前人表The prior-iGEM table

這一格在範例裡是空的，而且必須由搜尋填滿This cell is empty in the example, and search must fill it

這是 2026 這一季最明顯的程序缺口，也是本架構裡唯一不能靠記憶或推測完成的欄位。2027 的提案在二月必須交出一張有連結的表：促生菌與 ACC 脫胺酶、CcaS/CcaR 光控、B. subtilis 分泌、以及中空纖維或灌流硬體，這四個關鍵字各自搜過 Registry 與歷年 wiki，列出隊伍、年份、證明了什麼、沒證明什麼。

用搜尋結果做出的結論，才可以寫成新意陳述。沒有這張表的題目，在 PK 的 G2 一律最高 5 分。

This is the clearest procedural gap in the 2026 season, and the one column in this framework that cannot be completed from memory or inference. A 2027 pitch must hand over a linked table in February: four keyword sweeps — plant-growth-promoting bacteria and ACC deaminase, CcaS/CcaR optogenetics, B. subtilis secretion, and hollow-fibre or perfusion hardware — each searched across the Registry and the past wikis, listing team, year, what they proved and what they did not.

Only a conclusion built from that search may be written as a novelty statement. A project without the table scores no more than 5 on G2 at the PK.

新意陳述，與它的種類The novelty statement and its kind

種類：新整合 ＋ 新量測Kind: a new integration, plus a new method

新整合：光控的分泌迴路與中空纖維灌流分離分別都存在，而把它們接起來、並且在透膜液那一側同時量到蛋白與活性，這個交接點是我們要量的東西。新量測：我們自製的管線內 OD600 光度計在測量之前先把預期值寫進版本控制，再用實際跑出來的數據檢驗它——這在高中隊伍裡罕見，而且成本接近零。

New integration. An optogenetic secretion circuit and hollow-fibre perfusion separation both exist. Joining them and measuring both protein and activity on the permeate side is the handover we set out to quantify. New method. Our in-line OD600 photometer had its expected values committed to version control before the measurements, and was then tested against what the runs returned. That is rare in high-school iGEM and it costs almost nothing.

不宣稱：首創、世界第一、任何「唯一」。也不宣稱光控在反應器裡可用，除非 G5 第四列拿到數據。

Not claimed: first, world-first, only. Nor that light control works in the reactor, unless row four of the G5 table returns data.

後來What happened

「新量測」那一半完全兌現，而且變成全季最強的論證：八個預先登記的預測有五個被自己的數據推翻，而且那五個失敗被寫在頁面上。「新整合」那一半只兌現了一半：蛋白與活性都在透膜側量到了，但交接點的上游——綠光——從未進入反應器。至於 iGEM 前人表，整季都沒有做出來。

The "new method" half was kept in full and became the season's strongest argument: of eight pre-registered predictions, five were overturned by the team's own data, and those five failures were published. The "new integration" half was kept halfway: protein and activity were both measured on the permeate side, but the upstream of that handover — green light — never entered the reactor. The prior-iGEM table was never assembled at all.

量級估算Order of magnitude

二月必須寫出來的，是「一公頃一季需要多少酵素活性」對上「一台原型一天能做多少」，然後說出差幾個數量級。這個數字會決定十月被問到「產量夠嗎」時，是回答一個數字還是回答一段話。

February must put "how much enzyme activity one hectare needs in a season" against "how much one prototype makes in a day", and say how many orders of magnitude separate them. That number decides whether October answers "is the output enough?" with a figure or with a paragraph.

後來What happened

模型最後給出了每公頃 37.6 kU 的需求、81 L 的體積與約 US$3,800 的成本估計，而原型的硬體成本約 NT$83,028（約 US$2,800）。但兩個頁面對硬體定價不同，因為沒有人是那個數字的主人——這是後來才顯現的 G10 問題。產量那一側也始終沒有一個「每天多少」的數字，因為透膜側濃度在 12 小時達峰後下降，跑更久不等於收更多。這個特性如果在二月就寫進量級估算，整季對「產量」的敘述會不一樣。

The model eventually gave 37.6 kU per hectare, 81 L and about US$3,800, against a prototype hardware cost of about NT$83,028 (roughly US$2,800). Two pages then priced the hardware differently, because nobody owned the number — a G10 failure that only surfaced later. The output side never got a per-day figure either, because the permeate concentration peaks around 12 hours and falls, so a longer run does not mean a bigger harvest. Had that behaviour been written into the February estimate, the whole season's account of output would have read differently.

單點失敗，與它排在哪裡The single point of failure, and where it sits

第 3 節（分泌）是真正的單點失敗：蛋白出不來，後面四節全部不成立。第 2 節（光控）是題目的招牌，但它不是鏈的必要條件——組成型表現也能餵養第 4 到第 6 節。所以二月的正確排法是：

Link 3, secretion, is the real single point of failure: if the protein does not come out, the four links after it do not exist. Link 2, the light switch, is the headline but it is not load-bearing — constitutive expression can feed links 4 to 6. So February's correct ordering is:

  • 第 1 個月　第 3 節的最小版本：組成型啟動子 ＋ 兩種分泌訊號並列，看培養液裡有沒有東西。這一步不需要光，也不需要反應器。
  • 第 2–3 個月　第 2 節的 go／no-go：綠光對紅光的表現比，在光板上做，n＝3。這一步要在三月底有答案，因為若開關不成立，整季的敘述必須改成「組成型生產平台」，而那是一個完全可接受、但完全不同的題目。
  • 第 3–5 個月　第 4、5 節：反應器與透膜液。
  • 第 5–7 個月　第 6 節：植物。植物實驗的週期由種子決定，不能壓縮，所以要與反應器並行。
  • Month 1. The minimum version of link 3: a constitutive promoter plus two secretion signals side by side, asking only whether anything appears in the medium. No light, no reactor needed.
  • Months 2–3. The link-2 go/no-go: green against red expression on the light plate, n = 3. The answer is due by the end of March, because if the switch does not hold the season's account becomes "a constitutive production platform" — entirely acceptable, and an entirely different project.
  • Months 3–5. Links 4 and 5: the reactor and the permeate.
  • Months 5–7. Link 6: plants. The plant cycle is set by the seeds and cannot be compressed, so it runs in parallel with the reactor.

後來What happened

分泌那一節成立了，而且三個蛋白裡 ACC 脫胺酶的分泌量約為另外兩個的三倍，這個比較後來成為選擇主力蛋白的依據。反應器那兩節成立得很漂亮。但光控那一節——題目的招牌——排在了後面，整季每一個反應器批次都是組成型表現，而光控只在光板上測過，且在十月仍是「13 小時支持、16 小時反轉、n＝2、無統計」。如果三月底有一個 go／no-go，這一季的講法會從「我們的光控系統」變成「我們的連續生產平台，附帶一個尚未整合的光控模組」——後者更弱，但不會在評審面前塌。

Secretion held, and of the three proteins ACC deaminase was secreted at roughly three times the other two, a comparison that later decided which protein led. The two reactor links held well. The light link — the headline — was scheduled late, every reactor batch ran constitutive, and light control was tested only on the light plate, standing in October at "supported at 13 h, reversed at 16 h, n = 2, no statistic". With a go/no-go at the end of March the season's story would have shifted from "our optogenetic system" to "our continuous production platform, with a light module not yet integrated" — weaker, and it would not collapse in front of a judge.

人與紀錄People and the record

  • 每個實驗至少兩個人會做。2026 的教訓不是人手不足，是某些技術只有一個人會，於是那個人的考試週就是那條線的停擺週。
  • 紀錄值班：每週一人，把那一週變成一頁（日期、目的、材料、結果、照片、下一步）。這一項要寫進人力表，因為 2026 的實驗紀錄是在十月從聊天室與投影片倒回來重建的，那次重建花掉的時間比一整季的值班還多。
  • 生物安全：這個題目在 BSL-1 內。圍堵是物理的——0.2 µm 孔徑對上 0.25–1.0 µm 的菌體——不是基因線路，所以不需要自殺基因的逃脫率論證。
  • Every experiment has at least two people who can run it. The 2026 lesson was not a shortage of people; it was that some techniques had exactly one owner, so that person's exam week was that line's idle week.
  • A record rota: one person a week turns that week into one page — date, purpose, materials, result, photographs, next step. It belongs in the staffing table, because 2026's notebook was rebuilt in October out of chat logs and slide decks, and that rebuild cost more hours than a season of rota would have.
  • Biosafety: this project sits inside BSL-1. Containment is physical — a 0.2 µm pore against a 0.25–1.0 µm cell — not genetic, so no kill-switch escape-rate argument is needed.
後來What happened

資本那一側估得很準，2026 的硬體總成本落在原型約 NT$83,028（約 US$2,800）。借用那一側也成立。真正偏掉的是「同型批次 × 3」的耗材成本從未被編進去，於是 M6 永遠是 n＝1。另外，反應器料件清單後來發現漏了幾項（主控板、鹼液泵、攪拌子），這是二月清單不夠細的直接結果。

The capital side was estimated well: 2026's hardware came in at about NT$83,028 (roughly US$2,800) for the prototype. The borrowing side held. What went wrong is that the consumable cost of "three runs of the same kind" never entered the budget, so M6 stayed at n = 1. The reactor's parts list also turned out to be missing items — the mainboard, the base pump, the stir bar — a direct consequence of a February list that was not itemised finely enough.

軟體：它在實驗之前產出什麼預測Software: which prediction does it make before the experiment

算分的軟體Software that scores

  • 韌體層的安全連鎖：壓力超過門檻就停泵並閂鎖，重開機不會讓泵在無人的房間裡重啟。這是硬體能連續運轉數日的前提。
  • 預先登記的生長率與訊號處理：在量測之前把預期值寫進版本控制，然後用實跑的數據檢驗。推翻自己的那幾項，就是最強的證據。
  • 預測與異常偵測：在失效之前指出感測器要壞了。
  • Interlocks in firmware. Over the pressure threshold, both pumps stop and the fault latches, so a reboot cannot restart a pump in an empty room. This is what lets the hardware run unattended for days.
  • Pre-registered growth rate and signal processing. Expected values committed to version control before the measurement, then tested against what the runs returned. The predictions that were overturned are the strongest evidence of all.
  • Forecasting and anomaly detection. Saying a sensor is about to fail before it fails.

不算分的軟體Software that does not score

  • 把已經有的結果畫得好看的儀表板。
  • 只在錄好的數據上事後跑的模型。2026 的所有模型都是事後在錄製資料上跑的，頁面必須如實寫出這一點。
  • 使用者介面做得很完整，但沒有任何使用者試用過。
  • A dashboard that draws results you already have.
  • A model that only ever runs on recorded data after the fact. Every 2026 model did, and the page had to say so.
  • A polished user interface no user has tried.
後來What happened

韌體連鎖與預先登記兩項都兌現，並且撐起了量測類的論證：434 小時的管線內 OD 紀錄、以及異常偵測在 OD 通道失效前 26.7 小時就標記出參考通道。水耕板與多餘感測器則如上表所述，變成要在頁面上交代的項目。硬體一共做了四台加一個水耕板，比這套架構建議的「三台」多，而多出來的那些正是後來留下負債的那些。

The interlocks and the pre-registration were both delivered, and they carried the measurement argument: a 434-hour in-line OD record, and anomaly detection that flagged the reference channel 26.7 hours before the OD channel failed. The hydroponics plate and the spare sensors became the items the pages had to account for. Four instruments plus the plate were built, against this framework's suggested three — and the extras are precisely the ones that left debts.

三個停損條件Three kill criteria

各帶日期與數字門檻Each with a date and a numeric threshold
  1. 4 月 30 日　若培養液中仍無法在 ELISA 與西方墨點上同時偵測到任一目標蛋白，放棄分泌路線，改走胞內表現。
  2. 3 月 31 日　若綠／紅表現比在 n＝3 下低於 2，光控降級為模組，整季敘述改寫，並且不再把它放在簡報第一頁。
  3. 7 月 31 日　若透膜液與無細胞培養液對幼苗的效果在 n＝9 下無法區分，植物那一節改寫為方法學貢獻（一套可重複的逆境定量流程），不主張蛋白的功效。
  4. 30 April. If no target protein is detectable in the supernatant by both ELISA and blot, abandon secretion and move to intracellular expression.
  5. 31 March. If the green-to-red expression ratio is below 2 at n = 3, demote light control to a module, rewrite the season's account, and take it off the first slide.
  6. 31 July. If permeate and cell-free medium cannot be distinguished on seedlings at n = 9, rewrite the plant link as a methodological contribution — a repeatable stress-quantification protocol — and claim no efficacy for the protein.

縮小版題目The de-scoped version

只剩一半時間與一半資源時的最小可交付版本：一台管線內 OD600 光度計，完整校正、完整驗證、附組裝指南與料件清單，以及一條用它記錄的連續灌流培養曲線。這個版本沒有光控、沒有植物、沒有法規論述，但它是一個完整、誠實、可複製的成果，而且它是 2026 最強的那一塊。知道縮小版是什麼，就敢把關鍵實驗排在前面。

With half the time and half the resources, the minimum deliverable is: one in-line OD600 photometer, fully calibrated and validated, with an assembly guide and a costed parts list, plus one continuous perfusion growth curve recorded with it. No light control, no plants, no regulatory argument — and a complete, honest, reproducible result, which is also the strongest thing 2026 produced. Knowing what the de-scoped version is gives you the nerve to schedule the decisive experiment first.

後來What happened

2026 沒有寫停損條件，也因此沒有在三月問光控那一題。整季的結果是：縮小版（光度計與量測）無意間變成最強的那一塊，而招牌（光控）變成最弱的那一塊。這個排序如果在二月就被看見，簡報的第一頁會不一樣。

2026 wrote no kill criteria, and so never put the light question in March. The season ended with the de-scoped version — the photometer and the measurement work — as the strongest piece, and the headline, light control, as the weakest. Had that ordering been visible in February, the first slide would have been a different slide.

三個特別獎名額，以及為什麼只有三個The three Special Award slots, and why there are only three

2026 年的規則：一隊最多申請三個特別獎，三個之中至少一個來自 General Bio Engineering 類、至少一個來自 Specializations 類，而這三個就是金牌的條件——三個都要達到評分門檻才拿金牌，選不滿三個直接沒有金牌。所以「挑三個」不是策略建議，是規則。下面是 ReLEAF 的工作最該放的三個名額。

The 2026 rule: a team applies for at most three Special Awards, at least one from General Bio Engineering and at least one from Specializations, and those three are the Gold criterion — all three must meet the performance standard, and selecting fewer than three means no Gold at all. So picking three is not strategy, it is the rule. Below are the three slots ReLEAF's work belongs in.

論文：新意那一句話The paper: the novelty sentence

一台成本約 US$372 的管線內 OD600 光度計，其訊號處理在量測之前就被預先登記，並在真實的連續灌流培養中被自己的數據推翻五項預測；我們報告那五項失敗、修正後的閘門設計，以及由此得到的 434 小時無取樣濁度紀錄。

An in-line OD600 photometer costing about US$372, whose signal processing was pre-registered before measurement and then had five of its predictions overturned by its own data in a real continuous perfusion culture; we report those five failures, the gate redesign they forced, and the 434-hour sample-free turbidity record that followed.

種類：新方法。這是四種新意形狀裡高中隊伍最容易拿到的一種，因為它不需要新的生物學，只需要紀律。

Kind: a new method. Of the four novelty shapes this is the one a high-school team reaches most easily, because it needs no new biology, only discipline.

四到六張圖的清單：(1) 光學配置與反射率校正曲線；(2) 兩種質量標準品下與商用儀器的一致性，含斜率與截距檢定；(3) 預先登記的八項預測與實測結果對照；(4) 氣泡閘門開與關的誤差對比；(5) 434 小時連續紀錄，標出異常偵測早於失效的那個時點；(6) 外部自建版本的一致性。這份清單同時就是最低要完成的實驗清單。

The four-to-six figure list: (1) the optical layout and the reflectance calibration curve; (2) agreement with a commercial instrument on two mass standards, with the slope-and-intercept test; (3) the eight pre-registered predictions against what was measured; (4) error with the bubble gate on and off; (5) the 434-hour record with the point where anomaly detection preceded failure; (6) agreement for the outside-built version. That list is also the minimum experiment list.

每個數字一個主人One number, one owner

2026 有同一台硬體在兩頁上有兩個價錢，兩個都站得住，因為沒有人是那個數字的主人。二月就要指定：硬體成本、每公頃劑量、單位產量、菌株名稱、各自一位主人、一個來源檔案。所有頁面只能從那個檔案取數。

In 2026 the same hardware carried two prices on two pages, both defensible, because nobody owned the number. February assigns owners: hardware cost, dose per hectare, output per unit, strain name — one owner and one source file each. Every page draws from that file and nowhere else.

後來What happened

獎項那一側兌現得很好：量測、硬體、人文三個方向都有可以站得住的證據，而且零件那一項做到 35 個 Registry 登錄、五個可互換模組。論文那一句話事後回看也成立。沒做到的是數字主人：硬體價格、菌株名稱（WB800 與 WB800N 混用）、反應器產出區間（2.5–8.5 與 3.4–6.5 兩種說法）都在不同頁面上有不同版本，最後要靠一份判讀準備文件把它們統一。那份文件如果是二月的一張表，就不需要在十月寫。

The prize side came through: measurement, hardware and human practices all have evidence that stands up, and the parts work reached 35 Registry entries across five interchangeable modules. The novelty sentence holds up in hindsight too. What did not come through is number ownership: the hardware price, the strain name (WB800 and WB800N used interchangeably) and the reactor output window (quoted as both 2.5–8.5 and 3.4–6.5) each existed in more than one version across pages, and a judging-prep document had to reconcile them at the end. Had that document been a one-page table in February, October would not have had to write it.
