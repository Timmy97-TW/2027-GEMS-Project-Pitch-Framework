架構Framework

十道門 The ten gates

每一道門有三樣東西：它問的問題、二月前必須已經存在的證據、以及會讓它關上的紅旗。「必須存在」是指實體的東西——一張報價單、一條生長曲線、一次已經做完的訪談。計畫不算證據。

Each gate carries three things: the question it asks, the evidence that must already exist by February, and the red flags that close it. "Must exist" means something physical — a quote, a growth curve, an interview already held. A plan is not evidence.

階段一 · 28 分Phase Ⅰ · 28 points 值得做嗎Is it worth doing

這三道門答錯，代價是一季的熱情。它們很少擋掉題目，但會把題目改寫成能被評審聽懂的樣子。

Getting these three wrong costs a season of enthusiasm. They rarely reject a project outright; what they do is rewrite it into something a judge can follow.

G1

主張The claim

10

用一句可以被實驗否證的話說出這個題目，並說出它錯的時候數據長什麼樣子。

State the project as one sentence an experiment could falsify, and say what the data look like when it is wrong.

主張不是願景。「幫助小農對抗氣候變遷」不是主張，因為沒有任何一組數據能證明它錯。主張要長成這樣：某個被工程改造的系統，在某個條件下，讓某個可量測的量相對某個對照改變了多少。評審在兩分鐘內要能重述它。

A claim is not a vision. "Help smallholders face climate change" is not a claim, because no data set could refute it. A claim looks like this: an engineered system, under a stated condition, changes a measurable quantity by some amount against a named control. A judge must be able to repeat it back after two minutes.

二月前必須存在Must exist by February
  • 一句話主張，含單位與對照組，寫在提案第一頁第一行。
  • 一段「這個主張錯的時候」：描述失敗時的數據形狀，不是描述心情。
  • 三個版本的題目簡介：給評審的 25 字、給農民或使用者的 1 分鐘、給學校行政的 1 頁。三個版本不能互相矛盾。
  • The one-sentence claim, with units and a control, as the first line of page one.
  • A paragraph titled "when this claim is wrong" that describes the shape of the failing data, not a feeling about it.
  • Three descriptions: 25 words for a judge, one minute for a farmer or user, one page for the school. They must not contradict each other.
紅旗Red flags
  • 主張裡的動詞是「探討」、「建立平台」、「整合」。這些動詞沒有失敗模式。
  • 要兩句以上才講得完。那通常是兩個題目。
  • 主張講的是裝置，不是生物。iGEM 的主獎評的是工程化的生物系統。
  • The verb is "explore", "build a platform for", or "integrate". None of these has a failure mode.
  • It takes more than one sentence. That is usually two projects.
  • The claim is about a device, not about biology. iGEM's main awards judge an engineered biological system.
怎麼評分How it scores

10 分：一句話、有單位、有對照、有失敗形狀，三個版本一致。6 分：句子成立但對照組還沒決定。3 分：是一個研究方向而不是一個主張。0 分：無法否證。

10: one sentence, with units, a control and a failure shape; three descriptions agree. 6: the sentence holds but the control is undecided. 3: a research direction, not a claim. 0: unfalsifiable.

G2

前人與新意Prior art and the increment

10

誰已經做過這件事——文獻上和 iGEM 歷史上——而我們多出來的那一塊，是一句話講得完的嗎？

Who has already done this, in the literature and in iGEM's own history, and can the increment be said in one sentence?

iGEM 評審看過太多生物肥料、太多生物感測器、太多降解塑膠。重複不是問題，說不出自己多了什麼才是問題。這道門要產出兩張表：一張是文獻，一張是 iGEM 隊伍。第二張比第一張難查，也比第一張重要，因為評審裡會有人記得那些隊伍。

iGEM judges have seen a great many biofertilisers, biosensors and plastic degraders. Repetition is not the problem; being unable to name your own increment is. This gate produces two tables: one of literature, one of iGEM teams. The second is harder to assemble and matters more, because someone on the judging panel remembers those teams.

二月前必須存在Must exist by February
  • 40–60 篇略讀、10–15 篇細讀、3 篇錨論文。錨論文要寫出「我們相信它哪一張圖」。
  • iGEM 前人表：用 Registry 零件搜尋與歷年 wiki 搜尋做出來，每一列是「隊伍與年份／他們證明了什麼／他們沒證明什麼／我們因此改什麼」。這張表不能憑記憶寫，必須附連結。
  • 一句新意陳述，並標明它是哪一種新意：新的生物部件、新的量測方法、新的整合、新的使用場景、或新的成本結構。五種裡挑一種，不要宣稱五種。
  • 一份「我們不宣稱」清單：哪些話我們不會說（例如「首創」）。
  • 40–60 papers skimmed, 10–15 read closely, 3 anchor papers. For each anchor, state which figure you are trusting.
  • A prior-iGEM table, assembled from Registry part searches and past-wiki searches. One row per team: team and year / what they proved / what they did not / what we change because of it. This table may not be written from memory; every row carries a link.
  • One novelty sentence, labelled with its kind: a new biological part, a new measurement method, a new integration, a new deployment setting, or a new cost structure. Pick one of the five; do not claim all five.
  • A "we do not claim" list — the sentences you will refuse to say, "first ever" among them.
紅旗Red flags
  • iGEM 前人表是空的。那代表沒查，不代表沒人做過。
  • 新意建立在「我們用的生物不一樣」，但沒有理由說明為什麼那個生物更好。
  • 新意需要先解釋五分鐘才聽得懂。
  • The prior-iGEM table is empty. That means nobody searched, not that nobody has done it.
  • The increment is "we use a different organism", with no argument for why that organism is better here.
  • The increment needs five minutes of set-up before it lands.
怎麼查 iGEM 前人How to search iGEM prior art
  • Registry 零件搜尋：用蛋白名、基因名、啟動子名查，看誰註冊過，再從零件頁回溯到隊伍。
  • 歷年 wiki：用 site:2024.igem.wiki 關鍵字 的方式逐年搜，2015 年以前的在舊站 igem.org/Team:…，若打不開就用 Wayback Machine。
  • 每年的獎項名單：看同一個村落（Village）裡拿獎的題目，那是我們真正的比較組。
  • 查到的隊伍一律讀他們的 Results 與 Engineering 頁，不要只讀 Description。多數隊伍的 Description 承諾的比 Results 多。
  • Registry part search on the protein, gene and promoter names; from a part page, follow back to the team that submitted it.
  • Past wikis year by year: site:2024.igem.wiki <keyword>. Anything before about 2015 lives on the old igem.org/Team:… paths; use the Wayback Machine when those time out.
  • The award lists: look at what won inside the same Village. That is the real comparison group.
  • For every team found, read their Results and Engineering pages, not their Description. Most teams promise more on Description than they delivered on Results.
G3

影響與落地Impact and deployability

8

誰用它、誰付錢、我們交出去的是哪一個階段，下一個階段是誰的工作？

Who uses it, who pays for it, which stage do we hand over, and whose job is the stage after that?

落地不是宣稱產品會上市。落地是把階梯畫出來，然後誠實指出我們站在第幾階。評審反而會獎勵「我們交出的是第三階的原型，第四階需要田間試驗，而田間試驗需要某張許可，那張許可大約要多久」這種回答。

Deployability is not a claim that the product will reach market. It is drawing the ladder and saying honestly which rung you are on. A judge rewards the answer "we hand over a rung-three prototype; rung four is a field trial; the field trial needs this permit, and that permit takes about this long".

二月前必須存在Must exist by February
  • 落地階梯五階：實驗台 → 密閉原型 → 使用者現場試用 → 田間／真實場域試驗 → 法規與量產。標出十月我們會站在哪一階。
  • 使用者一句話：這個人是誰、他現在怎麼解決這個問題、我們省了他什麼（時間、錢、風險）。這句話要來自一次真的對話。
  • 量級估算（order-of-magnitude）：一個使用者單位一年需要多少、我們的原型一天做多少、差幾個數量級。差三個數量級不是致命，說不出差幾個才是。
  • A five-rung ladder: bench → contained prototype → user trial on site → field or real-setting trial → regulation and manufacture. Mark the rung you will be standing on in October.
  • One sentence about the user: who they are, how they solve this today, and what you remove for them — time, money or risk. The sentence comes out of a real conversation.
  • An order-of-magnitude estimate: what one user unit needs per year, what the prototype makes per day, and how many orders of magnitude apart those are. Being three orders out is survivable; not knowing the number is not.
紅旗Red flags
  • 市場規模用全球數字（「XX 億美元市場」），但沒有一個具體的使用者。
  • 階梯上我們宣稱會站到第四階，但 G6 的預算裡沒有田間試驗的錢。
  • The market is sized in billions and there is not one specific user.
  • The pitch claims rung four and the G6 budget has no money for a field trial.

階段二 · 42 分Phase Ⅱ · 42 points 我們做得到嗎Can this team actually do it

這三道門是這套架構的重心。答錯的代價是整季歸零：十月有一堆活動紀錄，沒有一組可以站得住的數據。

These three gates are the weight of the framework. Getting them wrong zeroes the season: October arrives with a great deal of activity and no data set that stands up.

G4

關鍵路徑The critical path

15

哪一個實驗失敗，整個題目就不成立？它排在第幾週，而不是第幾個？

Which single experiment, if it fails, makes the whole project untrue? In which week does it run — not in which position on the list?

多數高中隊伍把實驗按「容易到難」排，於是最關鍵的那一個排在最後，而最後那一段剛好是暑假結束、開學、wiki 凍結逼近的時候。正確的排法是把「失敗代價最高」的實驗排在最前面，哪怕它需要先等合成的 DNA 到貨。

Most high-school teams order experiments from easy to hard, which puts the decisive one last — and last falls in the weeks when school restarts and the wiki freeze is closing in. The right order puts the highest-cost-of-failure experiment first, even when it means waiting for synthesised DNA to arrive.

二月前必須存在Must exist by February
  • 依賴圖：每個實驗是一個節點，箭頭是「必須先有這個結果」。圖上標出最長鏈，那條鏈就是關鍵路徑。
  • 單點失敗實驗（single point of failure）明確命名，並排在第一個月。
  • 最長交期清單：DNA 合成、菌株或質體索取、抗體或套組、膜或特殊耗材、海關。每一項寫出「下單日」與「預期到貨日」。
  • 備援路徑：關鍵實驗若失敗，有沒有一個 B 方案能保住 50% 的題目？若沒有，這道門要降分，不是降分後照過。
  • 建構清單第一版：每個要做的質體，寫出骨架、啟動子、RBS、CDS、標籤、終止子，與來源（Registry 編號或 NCBI accession）。
  • A dependency graph: experiments as nodes, arrows meaning "this result must exist first". Mark the longest chain; that chain is the critical path.
  • The single point of failure named explicitly, and scheduled inside the first month.
  • A long-lead list: DNA synthesis, strain or plasmid requests, antibodies or kits, membranes or special consumables, customs. Each with an order date and an expected-arrival date.
  • A fallback: if the decisive experiment fails, is there a plan B that keeps half the project? If not, this gate loses points rather than passing with a note.
  • A first construct list: for every plasmid, the backbone, promoter, RBS, CDS, tags and terminator, each with a source — a Registry number or an NCBI accession.
紅旗Red flags
  • 題目的招牌功能（最常被講出來的那個）排在七月之後。
  • 依賴圖上有一個節點，它的上游是「跟某教授借菌株」，而這件事還沒問。
  • 關鍵路徑上有一步需要實驗室沒有的設備，而提案寫「可以外送」但沒有價格與週期。
  • The headline function — the one everyone says out loud — is scheduled after July.
  • A node's upstream is "borrow the strain from a professor" and nobody has asked yet.
  • A step on the critical path needs equipment the lab lacks, and the pitch says "we can outsource it" with no price and no turnaround.
為什麼這道門配 15 分Why this gate carries 15 points

因為它是唯一能在二月就買回時間的門。把關鍵實驗往前挪四個月，等於在九月多出四個月的修正空間；反之，九月才發現關鍵實驗做不出來，任何補救都只能改寫句子，不能改寫數據。

Because it is the only gate that buys time back in February. Moving the decisive experiment four months earlier gives you four months of correction in September. Discovering in September that it cannot be done leaves you rewriting sentences, which is not the same as having data.

G5

量測計畫The measurement plan

15

每一個主張對應哪一個數字、什麼單位、跟什麼對照比、做幾次、用哪台儀器、什麼時候做完？

For each claim: which number, in what unit, against which control, how many times, on which instrument, finished by when?

這張表是整份提案最重要的一頁。它決定十月有沒有東西可寫，也決定耗材要買多少。n＝1 的數據在 wiki 上看起來和 n＝3 一樣，但評審一問就塌，而且一旦做完就補不回來——細胞已經丟了，批次已經過去了。n 是在二月決定的，不是在八月。

This table is the most important page of the pitch. It decides whether there is anything to write in October, and it decides how many kits to buy. An n = 1 result looks the same on a wiki as an n = 3 result, and it collapses at the first question — and once the run is over it cannot be repaired, because the cells are gone and the batch is past. n is decided in February, not in August.

量測表的欄位。每一列是一個主張，不是一個實驗。The columns of the measurement table. One row per claim, not per experiment.

  • 每一列的 n 乘上單次成本，加總後要和 G6 的耗材預算對得上。
  • 一台儀器已經由學生自己校正過，並留下校正曲線。

  • The table above, filled: at least five rows, at most eight. More than eight means the project is too wide.
  • Each row's n multiplied by its unit cost, summed, reconciled against the G6 consumables budget.
  • One instrument already calibrated by the students, with the calibration curve kept.
  • A decision on pre-registration: which numbers get their expected values committed to version control before the run. It costs almost nothing and it is one of the few places a high-school team can genuinely lead.
紅旗Red flags
  • 表上有任何一列 n＝1，而那一列是主張的核心。
  • 對照欄有空格。
  • 所有統計都落在儀器上（校正、重現性），生物那一側沒有重複。
  • 量測全部依賴一台借來的設備，而借用只談過口頭。
  • Any row where n = 1 and that row is the core of the claim.
  • A blank in the control column.
  • All the statistics sit on the instrument — calibration, repeatability — and the biology has no replicates.
  • Everything depends on one borrowed instrument, and the loan is a verbal arrangement.
G6

資源：設備、錢、人Resources: equipment, money, people

12

做完 G5 那張表需要哪些設備與耗材，總共多少錢，誰來做，以及最長交期那一項什麼時候下單？

What equipment and consumables does the G5 table need, what does it cost in total, who runs it, and when is the longest-lead item ordered?

設備分三類，提案要逐項分類：已經有（在我們實驗室，現在可用）、要借（有具體單位、具體聯絡人、已經問過）、要買或要做（有價格、有交期）。第三類還要回答「買現成的多少錢、自己做多少錢、自己做的理由是什麼」。

Equipment falls into three classes, and the pitch sorts every item: have (in our lab, available now), borrow (a named institution, a named contact, already asked), buy or build (with a price and a lead time). Class three also answers: what does the commercial one cost, what does ours cost, and why are we building it.

二月前必須存在Must exist by February
  • 設備清單三類齊全，第三類每項有報價或網頁價格。
  • 耗材預算按月列，含 G5 的 n。合成 DNA、抗體與 ELISA 套組通常是三大項，要單獨列。
  • 30% 預備金，寫成一行，不是口頭。
  • 生物安全等級確認：這個題目需要 BSL-1 還是 BSL-2，我們的空間是哪一級，誰簽核。等級不符是否決項，不是保留項。
  • White List 核對（高中隊伍的硬限制）。iGEM 規定高中隊伍只能使用 White List 上的生物、零件與操作，因此不能碰需要 Check-in Form 或 Animal Use Form 的東西——實務上就是不做動物、不做人體樣本、不碰 BSL-2 以上的生物、不碰多數產孢真菌。這一題在二月要逐項對著 White List 核過。清單外的生物是否決項，不是保留項，而且它會在六月的安全表單被攔下來，不是在十月。
  • 人力表：每個實驗至少兩個人會做（一個人會做等於這個實驗有病假風險）。
  • 紀錄值班表：每週一人，把當週變成一頁。從第一週開始。
  • 技能缺口與補法：哪些技術全隊都還不會，誰教、什麼時候教。
  • The three-class equipment list, with a quote or a listed price for every class-three item.
  • A month-by-month consumables budget that carries the G5 replicate counts. Synthesised DNA, antibodies and ELISA kits are usually the three largest lines and get their own rows.
  • A 30% contingency, written as a line in the budget, not said out loud.
  • Biosafety level settled: does this project need BSL-1 or BSL-2, what is our space rated for, and who signs it off. A mismatch is a reject, not a hold.
  • A White List check, which is a hard limit on high-school teams. iGEM requires high-school teams to work only with organisms, parts and activities on the White List, so anything needing a Check-in Form or an Animal Use Form is out: in practice no animals, no human samples, nothing at BSL-2 or above, and most sporulating fungi excluded. In February, check every organism against the White List item by item. An organism off the list is a reject, not a hold, and it is caught by the June safety form rather than in October.
  • A staffing table where every experiment has at least two people who can run it. One person able to run it means the experiment has a sick-day risk.
  • A record-keeping rota: one person a week turns that week into one page. From week one.
  • A skills gap list: which techniques nobody on the team can do yet, who teaches them, and when.
紅旗Red flags
  • 預算裡沒有運費、關稅、或低溫運送。生物材料的運費常常比材料貴。
  • 關鍵量測依賴一台要借的儀器，而借用還沒問過。
  • 提案假設學生暑假全天在實驗室，但沒有和家長或學校確認過。
  • No shipping, duty or cold-chain line in the budget. For biological material the shipping often exceeds the material.
  • A decisive measurement depends on a borrowed instrument and nobody has asked for it.
  • The plan assumes students are in the lab full time over the summer, and neither the parents nor the school have been asked.

階段三 · 15 分Phase Ⅲ · 15 points 範圍Scope

這兩道門處理同一件事：題目會不自覺地長大。硬體會長出第二台，軟體會長出一個介面，訪談會長成一份報告。每一個都不算錯，加起來會把濕實驗的時間吃掉。

Both gates handle the same thing: projects grow on their own. Hardware grows a second instrument, software grows an interface, interviews grow into a report. None of that is wrong, and together it eats the wet lab's time.

G7

硬體與軟體範圍Hardware and software scope

8

每一台儀器、每一支程式，少了它哪一個量測做不到？答不出來的，就不要做。

For every instrument and every program: which measurement is impossible without it? If there is no answer, do not build it.

硬體與軟體在 iGEM 是加分項，但只有一種硬體真的加分：讓某個量測變得可能的硬體。做來示範的硬體會被評審識破，而且會在頁面上留下「裝了但未測」這種句子。同一條規則適用於軟體：如果模型只是把實驗數據畫得好看，它不是模型；如果模型在實驗前給出預測、而且那個預測可能被推翻，它才是。

Hardware and software earn points in iGEM, but only one kind of hardware really does: hardware that makes a measurement possible. Hardware built to demonstrate gets seen through, and it leaves sentences like "fitted, not tested" on the page. The same rule covers software: a model that draws the experiment's data prettily is not a model. A model that makes a prediction before the run, one that could be overturned, is.

二月前必須存在Must exist by February
  • 每一項硬體一行：它讓哪一個量測成為可能、市售替代品價格、我們自製成本、以及「如果不做它，哪一列 G5 的表會空掉」。
  • 自製硬體要附一條驗證路徑：拿什麼標準品、跟哪一台商用儀器比、可接受的差異是多少。沒有驗證路徑的自製儀器產生的數字不能用。
  • 軟體一行：它在實驗之前產出什麼預測，以及那個預測被推翻時我們怎麼寫。
  • 不做清單：這一季明確不做的硬體與軟體，寫出來。
  • One line per hardware item: which measurement it makes possible, the price of the commercial alternative, our build cost, and which row of the G5 table goes blank without it.
  • Every self-built instrument carries a validation route: which standard, which commercial instrument it is compared against, and what difference is acceptable. Numbers from an unvalidated home-built instrument cannot be used.
  • One line per software item: which prediction it makes before the experiment, and how we will write it up when that prediction is overturned.
  • A not-doing list: the hardware and software this season explicitly will not build.
紅旗Red flags
  • 硬體的理由是「比較便宜」，但沒有任何量測因此才做得到。便宜本身是好事，不是題目。
  • 軟體是一個 app，它的功能是展示我們已經知道的結果。
  • 硬體清單超過三件。一個高中隊伍把三台儀器做到可驗證已經很好。
  • The reason for the hardware is "it is cheaper" and no measurement becomes possible because of it. Cheap is good; it is not a project.
  • The software is an app whose function is to display results you already have.
  • The hardware list runs past three items. Three instruments taken to the point of validation is already a strong season for a high-school team.
G8

人文實踐與法規Practices and regulation

7

哪些對話會真的改掉設計，而不是確認設計？我們走哪一條法規路徑，那條路徑的時鐘多長？

Which conversations will change the design rather than confirm it? Which regulatory route are we on, and how long is its clock?

整合式人文實踐的評分標準只有一條：那場對話改了什麼。一次訪談如果只讓我們更有信心，它不算整合，它算宣傳。二月要做的不是排滿訪談，是找出「哪三個問題我們自己回答不了，必須問人」，然後去問。

Integrated human practices is scored on one thing: what the conversation changed. An interview that only increased your confidence is not integration, it is publicity. February's job is not a full interview calendar; it is naming the three questions the team cannot answer for itself, and going to ask them.

二月前必須存在Must exist by February
  • 三場已經做完的對話，每一場留下一行「他說了什麼／我們因此改了什麼」。其中一位是潛在使用者，不是只有教授。
  • 三個我們自己答不出來的問題，與要去問的人名單。
  • 法規路徑：這個產品或技術在台灣屬於哪一類（例如肥料、生物農藥、醫療器材、食品添加），對應的法源名稱，以及該類別大約的審查時程。類別選擇本身就是一個設計決定。
  • 圍堵策略一句話：工程菌會不會離開容器？如果不會，是什麼物理結構擋住它？如果會，是什麼生物機制限制它？iGEM 的安全審查會問這一題。
  • 安全與保安表單的負責人與送交日期（iGEM 每季有自己的期限，照當季公告）。
  • Three conversations already held, each leaving one line: what they said, what we changed. One of the three is a potential user, not only professors.
  • The three questions the team cannot answer itself, with the names of the people to ask.
  • The regulatory route: which category this falls into in Taiwan — fertiliser, biopesticide, medical device, food additive — the statute that governs it, and roughly how long that category's review takes. The choice of category is itself a design decision.
  • Containment in one sentence: do the engineered cells leave the vessel? If not, which physical structure stops them? If so, which biological mechanism limits them? iGEM's safety review asks this.
  • An owner and a submission date for the Safety & Security forms, following the current season's published deadlines.
紅旗Red flags
  • 訪談清單上全部是教授。教授會告訴你實驗怎麼做，不會告訴你誰願意付錢。
  • 法規那一欄寫「未來需要申請相關許可」。那是空白的另一種寫法。
  • 圍堵靠的是「自殺基因」，但沒人查過逃脫率的文獻數量級。
  • Every name on the interview list is a professor. Professors tell you how to run the experiment; they do not tell you who will pay.
  • The regulation column says "relevant permits will be required in future". That is a blank with more words.
  • Containment rests on a kill switch and nobody has looked up the order of magnitude of published escape rates.

階段四 · 15 分Phase Ⅳ · 15 points 收割Harvest

做完了不等於拿到分。這兩道門處理「同樣的工作怎麼換到最多的獎項與一篇論文」，以及「什麼時候該承認這個題目不行」。

Finished is not the same as scored. These two gates handle how the same work converts into the most awards and one paper, and when to admit the project is not working.

G9

風險與停損Risk and kill criteria

8

前五大風險各由哪一個實驗、在哪一個日期解決？以及：什麼情況下我們會停掉這個題目？

Which experiment resolves each of the top five risks, and on what date? And: under what circumstances do we stop this project?

幾乎沒有 iGEM 隊伍寫停損條件，因為寫了像是不夠投入。實際上相反：有停損條件的隊伍敢把關鍵實驗排在前面，因為失敗有退路。沒有停損條件的隊伍會把關鍵實驗一直往後推，因為一旦失敗就沒有劇本。

Almost no iGEM team writes kill criteria, because writing them looks like a lack of commitment. The opposite holds: a team with kill criteria dares to schedule the decisive experiment first, because failure has a route out. A team without them keeps pushing that experiment back, because failure has no script.

二月前必須存在Must exist by February
  • 風險表五列：風險、發生機率（高／中／低）、影響、解決它的實驗、日期、失敗後的動作。
  • 三個停損條件，各帶日期與數字門檻。例如「若四月底仍無法在培養液中偵測到目標蛋白，放棄分泌路線，改走細胞內表現加破菌」。
  • 縮小版題目（de-scoped version）：如果只剩一半的時間和一半的資源，這個題目的最小可交付版本是什麼？這個版本在二月就要寫出來，不是在八月慌亂時才想。
  • A five-row risk table: risk, likelihood (high/medium/low), impact, the experiment that resolves it, the date, and the action if it fails.
  • Three kill criteria, each with a date and a numeric threshold. For example: "if the target protein is still undetectable in the culture medium by the end of April, abandon the secretion route and move to intracellular expression with lysis".
  • A de-scoped version: with half the time and half the resources, what is the minimum deliverable project? Write it in February, not in the August panic.
紅旗Red flags
  • 風險表上最大的風險是「時間管理」。
  • 停損條件沒有數字門檻，只有「如果效果不好」。
  • The largest risk on the table is "time management".
  • A kill criterion with no numeric threshold, only "if it does not work well".
G10

里程碑、獎項、論文Milestones, prizes, paper

7

四個 go／no-go 日期是什麼？我們瞄準哪三個特別獎，各需要什麼證據？論文的新意是哪一句？

What are the four go/no-go dates? Which three special prizes are we aiming at, and what evidence does each need? Which sentence is the paper's novelty?

獎項不是做完才去挑的，而且在 iGEM 的規則下根本不可能事後挑：一隊最多只能申請三個特別獎，而金牌的條件就是那三個。2026 年的規則寫得很清楚——三個之中至少一個來自「General Bio Engineering」類、至少一個來自「Specializations」類；而且金牌不再另外投票，是由那三個獎的評分推導出來的，三個都要達到門檻才算金牌。選少於三個就直接失去金牌資格。

Prizes are not chosen after the work, and under iGEM's rules they cannot be: a team may apply for at most three Special Awards, and those three are the Gold medal criterion. The 2026 rules are explicit — at least one of the three from the General Bio Engineering category and at least one from Specializations; and Gold is no longer a separate vote, it is inferred from the scores on those three awards, all three of which must meet the performance standard. Selecting fewer than three forfeits Gold outright.

這把「選獎」從收尾工作變成二月的設計決定。每個特別獎的評分標準會往回改掉實驗怎麼做：瞄準量測類，就要在二月決定買標準品、做預先登記；瞄準硬體類，就要在二月決定每台儀器都附組裝指南與料件清單。先選獎、再排實驗，成本幾乎是零；做完再想，只能補寫文字。

That turns award selection from a tidying-up job into a February design decision. Each award's criteria reach back and change how the experiments run: aim at a measurement award and February buys standards and commits a pre-registration; aim at a hardware award and February decides every instrument ships with an assembly guide and a costed parts list. Choosing first and ordering the experiments afterwards costs almost nothing. Choosing afterwards leaves you writing text.

論文那一句話怎麼寫Writing the paper's novelty sentence

iGEM 的結果要變成論文，缺的通常不是數據量，是對照與重複。一個可投稿的最小包是：一個明確的生物主張、n≥3 的核心數據、一個陰性與一個陽性對照、一個獨立方法的交叉驗證（例如同一件事用西方墨點和酵素活性各測一次）、以及一份別人照著能重做的方法。

新意那一句話的四種常見形狀：(1) 新部件——我們做出並定性了一個先前沒有被定性的元件；(2) 新方法——我們的量測或分析讓別人以後能便宜地做同一件事；(3) 新整合——先前分別存在的 A 與 B，第一次在同一個系統裡交接，而且我們量到了交接點；(4) 新場景——同樣的生物放在一個先前沒人放的環境裡，而環境改變了結論。第 (2) 與 (3) 是高中隊伍最容易拿到的。

Turning iGEM results into a paper usually fails on controls and replicates, not on volume. A minimum submittable package: one clear biological claim, core data at n ≥ 3, one negative and one positive control, a cross-check by an independent method (the same thing measured once by blot and once by enzyme activity), and a methods section someone else can repeat.

The novelty sentence takes four common shapes. (1) A new part: we built and characterised an element nobody had characterised. (2) A new method: our measurement or analysis lets others do the same thing cheaply. (3) A new integration: A and B existed separately, and for the first time they hand over inside one system, and we measured the handover. (4) A new setting: the same biology placed somewhere nobody had placed it, and the setting changed the conclusion. Shapes 2 and 3 are the ones a high-school team reaches most easily.
