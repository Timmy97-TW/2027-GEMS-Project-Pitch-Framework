開跑包Kick-off

兩份文件：題目 brief，與 12 月 12 日的說明 Two documents: the project brief, and the 12 December handout

老師寫的題目和學生自己提的題目，用同一張 brief 模板——PK 才公平。12 月 12 日那份說明是發給新成員的，講清楚這一季怎麼運作、第一天就要知道的四條規則、以及要怎麼記錄。

The instructors' projects and the students' own projects fill in the same brief, which is what makes the PK fair. The 12 December handout goes to new members: how the season works, the four rules everyone needs on day one, and how the record gets kept.

題目 brief：一頁，十二格The project brief: one page, twelve boxes

這張模板寫的是問題，不是解法。老師開題目的時候最容易犯的錯，是把 brief 寫成已經想好的方案，於是學生只剩下執行，沒有空間思考。好的 brief 把問題、限制與「最少要看到什麼」講清楚，解法留白。

This template describes a problem, not a solution. The usual mistake when an instructor writes a brief is to write a plan that is already decided, leaving students execution and no room to think. A good brief states the problem, the constraints and the minimum result, and leaves the solution blank.

同一張模板，兩種用法One template, two uses
  • 老師開題：填 1–8 格，第 9–12 格留白給學生填。這是「問題 brief」。
  • 學生提案：十二格全部自己填。這是「PK 提案」。
  • 兩種都不得超過一頁。超過一頁的 brief，房間裡沒有人會讀完。
  • Instructor's brief: fill boxes 1–8 and leave 9–12 for the students. This is a problem brief.
  • Student's pitch: fill all twelve. This is a PK proposal.
  • Neither runs past one page. Nobody in the room finishes a brief that does.
題目 brief 模板。右欄是寫的時候要避開的東西。The project brief template. The right-hand column is what to avoid while writing it.
格Box 要寫什麼What goes in it 不要寫成這樣What not to write
1 問題The problem兩句話。誰遇到這個問題，以及他們現在怎麼處理。Two sentences: who has this problem and how they handle it today. 用全球統計開場而沒有一個具體的人。Opening on a global statistic with no specific person in it.
2 為什麼現在的做法不夠Why today's answer falls short現有方案的具體缺口：太貴、太慢、要冷鏈、需要專業人員。The concrete gap: too expensive, too slow, needs a cold chain, needs a trained operator. 「目前尚無有效解決方案」——幾乎總是不實。"No effective solution currently exists" — almost always untrue.
3 合成生物學在這裡能做什麼What synthetic biology can do here一句話說出生物在這個系統裡的角色。One sentence on what the biology's job is inside the system. 生物只是裝置裡的配角。iGEM 的主獎評的是工程化的生物系統。The biology as a supporting part inside a device. iGEM's main awards judge an engineered biological system.
4 三篇錨論文Three anchor papers每篇一行：我們相信它哪一張圖，以及它不保證什麼。One line each: which figure we trust, and what it does not promise. 只給 DOI 清單，沒有說相信它什麼。A list of DOIs with no statement of what is being trusted.
5 實驗室做不做得到Can this lab do it底盤、生物安全等級、關鍵設備、缺什麼。高中隊伍要先對過 White List。Chassis, biosafety level, the key equipment, what is missing. A high-school team checks the White List first. 留白。這一格留白等於這個題目還沒被評估過。Left blank. A blank here means the project has not been assessed at all.
6 過去的 iGEM 隊伍做過什麼What past iGEM teams did至少三隊，附連結，各寫「證明了什麼／沒證明什麼」。At least three teams, with links, each with what they proved and what they did not. 憑印象寫「好像沒人做過」。"I don't think anyone has done this", written from impression.
7 八月前最少要看到的一個結果The one result we must see by August一個數字、一個單位、一個對照。這一格是整張 brief 的重心。One number, one unit, one control. This box is the weight of the whole brief. 「成功表現目標蛋白」——沒有單位就不是結果。"Successfully express the target protein" — with no unit it is not a result.
8 最明顯的失敗方式The most obvious way this fails老師自己先講。這一格的誠實程度決定學生敢不敢挑戰這個題目。The instructor says it first. How honest this box is decides whether students dare to challenge the project. 「時間不夠」。那是每個題目的答案。"Not enough time." That is every project's answer.
9 脊椎The spine輸入 → … → 輸出，五到七節，每節要交給下一節什麼。Input → … → output, five to seven links, each handing something to the next. 兩條各自獨立的鏈。那是兩個題目。Two independent chains. That is two projects.
10 單點失敗，排在第幾週The single point of failure, and its week哪一個實驗失敗，整個題目不成立。Which experiment, if it fails, makes the project untrue. 排在九月。Scheduled in September.
11 五到八列量測表Five to eight measurement rows主張、數字與單位、對照、n、儀器、完成日。Claim, number and unit, control, n, instrument, done-by. 對照欄有空格，或任何一列 n＝1。A blank in the control column, or any row at n = 1.
12 提案人的驗屍The proposer's pre-mortem「十月失敗最可能的原因是＿，我用＿實驗在＿日期排除它」。"The most likely reason this has failed by October is ＿; I rule it out with ＿ on ＿." 寫得比第 8 格樂觀。More optimistic than box 8.
老師寫 brief 的三條規矩Three rules for writing a brief
  1. 第 7 格先寫。先寫出八月前最少要看到的那個數字，再回頭寫問題。順序顛倒過來寫的 brief，幾乎都會變成一個講得很好但量不出來的故事。
  2. 第 8 格要比學生想得到的更誠實。學生會在 PK 當天問出這一題；由你自己先寫出來，題目的可信度反而更高。
  3. 解法留白。第 9 到 12 格是學生的工作。老師如果連這四格都填好了，學生加入的是一個專案，不是一個題目。
  4. Write box 7 first. Start from the number that must exist by August and work back to the problem. Briefs written the other way round nearly always become a story that tells well and cannot be measured.
  5. Make box 8 more honest than the students would manage. They will ask this on PK day. Writing it yourself makes the project more credible, not less.
  6. Leave the solution blank. Boxes 9 to 12 are the students' work. An instructor who fills those too has recruited people into a project rather than offering them a problem.

12 月 12 日，給新成員12 December, for new members

歡迎加入。這一頁講三件事：這一季怎麼運作、第一天就要知道的四條規則、以及你的工作要怎麼被記下來。讀完大概十分鐘。

Welcome. This page covers three things: how the season works, the four rules you need on day one, and how your work gets recorded. About ten minutes to read.

這一季怎麼運作How the season works

  • 12 月 12 日 → 1 月 9 日12 Dec → 9 Jan

    基本訓練，兩條線一起跑Basic training, both tracks at once

    濕實驗每週一個技術：無菌操作與配製培養基、PCR 與電泳、組裝與轉形、表現與量測。每一次都要寫紀錄。乾實驗同時進行，你會依興趣選一條：建模、硬體、網站、或訪談，每個人做出一個小成品。訓練的目的不只是學技術，也是讓你知道自己適合哪一條線。

    One wet-lab technique a week: sterile technique and media, PCR and gels, assembly and transformation, expression and measurement. Each one gets written up. The dry-lab track runs at the same time and you pick one by interest — modelling, hardware, the website, or interviews — and make one small thing. The training is not only about technique; it is how you find out which track suits you.

  • 12 月 19 日 → 1 月 23 日19 Dec → 23 Jan

    題目探索：加入一個，或自己提一個Project exploration: join one, or propose your own

    老師會提出一到三個題目 brief。你可以選一份深入，也可以自己提一個題目來 PK。兩條路的要求完全一樣：同一張 brief 模板、同一張評分表。老師的題目也會被學生打分。

    The instructors put forward one to three project briefs. You can go deep on one of them, or propose your own and take it to the PK. Both routes carry the same requirement: the same brief template and the same scorecard. The instructors' projects are scored by the students too.

  • 1 月下旬Late January

    PK 日PK day

    每個題目唸一頁提案、回答「假設十月失敗了，最可能的原因是什麼」、接受提問。每個人替每一個題目獨立打分，再一起討論分數差距最大的地方。

    Each project reads its one-pager, answers "suppose this has failed by October, what is the most likely reason", and takes questions. Everyone scores every project independently, then the room discusses wherever the scores are furthest apart.

  • 農曆年前（2 月 6 日是除夕）Before Lunar New Year, 6 Feb

    定案，然後寒假就開工Commitment, and the holiday is the first sprint

    全隊集中在一個題目上。年前定案的理由很實際：合成 DNA 和索取質體要等好幾週，寒假那幾週剛好是該下單的時候。

    The whole team moves onto one project. The reason for deciding before the holiday is practical: synthesised DNA and plasmid requests take weeks to arrive, and those holiday weeks are exactly when they should be ordered.

定案前的輕量預習Light preparation before the commitment

不要求全部做完。但交作業的情況是我們判斷誰投入的主要依據，而且這些作業之後會直接變成提案的一部分。

None of this is compulsory. What gets handed in is the main thing we go on when judging commitment, and these pieces become parts of the pitch later anyway.

作業一Assignment one

拆解一支得獎隊伍Take apart an award-winning team

挑一支過去的 iGEM 得獎隊伍，用固定模板寫一頁：他們的問題是什麼、用什麼底盤、實際證明了什麼、哪裡弱。讀他們的 Results 與 Engineering 頁，不要只讀 Description——多數隊伍的 Description 承諾的比 Results 多。

Pick a past award-winning iGEM team and write one page to a fixed template: their problem, their chassis, what they actually proved, where they were weak. Read their Results and Engineering pages, not their Description — most teams promise more on Description than they delivered.

作業二Assignment two

前人搜尋The prior-art sweep

選一個你有興趣的題目方向，用 Registry 零件搜尋加上歷年 wiki 搜尋（site:2024.igem.wiki 關鍵字），做出一張附連結的表：隊伍、年份、證明了什麼、沒證明什麼。這份作業最能看出一個題目有沒有新意。

Pick a direction that interests you and build a linked table from Registry part searches plus past-wiki searches (site:2024.igem.wiki <keyword>): team, year, what they proved, what they did not. This is the assignment that shows most clearly whether a project has an increment.

基礎Fundamentals

生物的最小集合The minimum biology

中心法則；promoter、RBS、CDS、terminator 各自在做什麼；質體是什麼；PCR、電泳、轉形、Gibson 或 Golden Gate 組裝。Addgene 的 Plasmids 101 是免費的，很適合入門。

The central dogma; what a promoter, an RBS, a CDS and a terminator each do; what a plasmid is; PCR, gels, transformation, and Gibson or Golden Gate assembly. Addgene's Plasmids 101 is free and a good way in.

工具Tools

四樣要會用的東西Four things to be able to use

Benchling（畫質體、設計引子）、PubMed 與 Google Scholar、Zotero（文獻管理），以及怎麼讀一篇論文：先看圖，再看 methods，最後才看 discussion。

Benchling for drawing plasmids and designing primers, PubMed and Google Scholar, Zotero for references, and how to read a paper: figures first, then methods, and discussion last.

第一天就要知道的四條規則Four rules you need on day one

一　實驗室安全One · Lab safety

沒有受過該項訓練就不要動那項操作，即使你看過別人做。任何溢出、割傷、燙傷、或疑似污染，當下就講，不要等到實驗結束。這不是責任歸屬問題，是後面所有數據還能不能用的問題。

Do not do an operation you have not been trained on, even if you have watched someone do it. Any spill, cut, burn or suspected contamination gets said out loud at the time, not after the experiment. This is not about blame; it is about whether the data after it can still be used.

二　iGEM 的 White List（高中隊伍的硬限制）Two · The iGEM White List, a hard limit on high-school teams

iGEM 規定高中隊伍只能使用 White List 上的生物、零件與操作。實務上就是：不做動物、不做人體樣本、不碰 BSL-2 以上的生物、不碰多數產孢真菌。這會直接決定哪些題目可以提——所以提案之前先去看一次 White List。清單外的生物不是「再想辦法」，是換題目。

iGEM requires high-school teams to work only with organisms, parts and activities on the White List. In practice: no animals, no human samples, nothing at BSL-2 or above, and most sporulating fungi excluded. This decides which projects can even be proposed, so read the White List before you pitch. An organism off the list is not a problem to work around; it is a different project.

三　AI 怎麼用Three · How AI is used
  • AI 可以潤飾、翻譯、整理、幫你讀懂一段論文。
  • 內容由團隊撰寫。你沒做過的實驗、沒讀過的文獻，不能讓 AI 替你寫成好像做過。
  • 證據圖永遠不能用 AI 產生或「修整」。膠圖、墨點、培養皿照片、儀器曲線都是證據，調整亮度對比都不行。
  • 用了就要揭露。這是 iGEM 的要求，也是我們的習慣。
  • AI may polish, translate, tidy, and help you understand a paper.
  • The team authors the content. An experiment you did not run or a paper you did not read cannot be written up by AI as though you had.
  • Evidentiary figures are never AI-generated or AI-"cleaned". Gels, blots, plate photographs and instrument traces are evidence; adjusting brightness or contrast is not allowed either.
  • Where it is used, it is disclosed. That is iGEM's requirement and our habit.
四　紀錄Four · The record

做了但沒記下來的實驗，到十月等於沒做。每個實驗一篇貼文，固定格式：日期、目的、材料與批號、步驟上的偏差、結果、照片、下一步。失敗的實驗也要記——失敗的紀錄是後面能不能找出原因的唯一材料，而且在 iGEM 的評分裡，誠實的失敗紀錄是加分的。

An experiment that happened but was not written down did not happen, as far as October is concerned. One post per experiment, in a fixed shape: date, purpose, materials and lot numbers, where you departed from the protocol, result, photographs, next step. Failed experiments get written up too — the record of a failure is the only material you have for working out why, and in iGEM's judging an honest failure record earns marks.

Discord 怎麼用How we use Discord

我們留在 Discord。真正要改的不是用哪一個聊天工具，是不要讓聊天記錄成為唯一的紀錄。去年十月，整份實驗紀錄是從聊天室和投影片倒回來重建的，那次重建花掉的時間比一整季的值班還多。

We are staying on Discord. The thing to change is not which chat tool we use; it is not letting the chat be the only record. Last October the whole notebook had to be rebuilt out of chat logs and slide decks, and that rebuild cost more hours than a season of keeping it would have.

  • 實驗用 forum 頻道，一個實驗一篇貼文Experiments go in forum channels, one post per experiment貼文有固定模板，討論留在那篇貼文的串裡，不要散到別的頻道。The post follows a template, and the discussion stays in that post's thread instead of scattering.
  • #decisions 頻道只放決定#decisions holds decisions only不聊天。每一條寫：決定了什麼、為什麼、誰拍板、日期。這個頻道到十月會變成人文實踐與工程頁的骨架。No chat. Each line: what was decided, why, who decided, the date. By October this channel becomes the skeleton of the human practices and engineering pages.
  • 原始數據放雲端硬碟，不要只貼圖Raw data goes to the drive, not only as an image檔名有規則：日期_實驗_條件_重複。聊天室裡的截圖不能重新分析。Filenames follow a rule: date_experiment_condition_replicate. A screenshot in a chat cannot be re-analysed.
  • 每週五有人值班，把那一週整理成一頁Someone is on rota each Friday to turn the week into one page輪流。一週一小時，一整季下來就是一份完整的實驗紀錄。Rotating. One hour a week, and the season ends with a complete notebook.

你會被怎麼看What we are looking for

不是看誰生物學最好。在這個階段我們看三件事：交出來的東西（哪怕很粗糙）、問出來的問題（尤其是挑戰老師題目的那種）、以及紀錄寫不寫得清楚。這三件事和你現在會多少技術沒有關係，而且都是練得起來的。

Not who knows the most biology. At this stage we watch three things: what you hand in, however rough; the questions you ask, especially the ones that challenge an instructor's project; and whether your records are clear enough for someone else to follow. None of the three depends on what you can already do, and all three can be practised.
